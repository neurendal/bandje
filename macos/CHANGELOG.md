# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-11)

Markers (also afterwards, with notes), one visible player, video import, and the window opening after a recording or a menu action.

- Band·je is free (App Store, `../specs/purchase.md`): recording is always free and never limited; transcribing is free for the first 5 recordings (was 3 before release; a recording counts once, when its first transcription finishes) and dictation for 15 minutes in total (was 60 before release: dictated prompts are short). Once 10 minutes are used, a small note says once how many free minutes are left, after the text is pasted, with **See…** for the dictation paywall; while few minutes are left the menu bar menu shows them under **Start Dictation**. Two one-time purchases, no subscription: **Band·je Unlimited** (transcribing and dictation without limit) and **Band·je Dictation** (dictation only). Whoever bought Band·je before it was free keeps everything. What you already have (transcripts, summaries, sharing, export) is never locked. Past the limit, **Transcribe** and the dictation shortcut show a small paywall; an automatic transcription doesn't start and the recording shows a short line instead. Under **Transcribe** a caption says how many free ones are left. **Settings › Purchases** shows what is unlocked, both products, **Restore Purchases** and **Redeem Code…**; the Band·je menu has **Buy…**, **Restore Purchases** and **Redeem Code…** after Settings. A build from source has everything unlocked and no store. Help: new page Band·je Unlimited.
- The welcome window (App Store build) has a **Free to start** section after the shortcuts: recording is always free, the first 5 transcriptions and 15 minutes of dictation are free, then Band·je Unlimited or Band·je Dictation, one payment, no subscription; with **Already bought it? Restore Purchases**. When everything is unlocked (bought, or bought before it was free) it only says "Everything is unlocked. Thank you!"; with Band·je Dictation it says Dictation is unlocked and unlimited transcribing comes with Band·je Unlimited. While the App Store hasn't said yet whether you bought Band·je before it was free (everything is unlocked meanwhile), the welcome shows what is free instead of claiming Band·je Dictation is unlocked, Settings › Purchases says "Everything is unlocked." without naming a purchase, and Restore Purchases only says "Purchases restored" for what the App Store confirmed.
- The welcome window has two steps in one window of the same size (fits a 13-inch MacBook without scrolling, with or without the store): **Getting to know** (the cassette, the shortcuts, Free to start, Made with love by Neurendal) with **Next**, then **Permissions** (a check-mark header, the four permissions and the privacy line) with **Back** and **Start** ("You can allow this later." under it). **Open Band·je at login** is under both steps. Return is Next/Start, Esc or ⌘[ goes back; two small dots show the step. After an update with a permission missing, the welcome opens on Permissions.
- The App Store build no longer pastes dictated text for you (App Review 2.4.5, rejected again on 2026-11-19: a permission listed under Accessibility may only serve accessibility). Dictated text goes to the clipboard and you paste it with ⌘V; ⌘V shows next to the menu bar cassette for 3 seconds after each dictation. The App Store build never sends key events and never asks for the Accessibility (post event) permission: **Turn On Automatic Pasting…** is gone from the menu, the welcome and Settings › Access. A build from source still has automatic pasting as described below. Help (10 languages) describes the clipboard way only.
- Automatic pasting (build from source only since 2026-11-20) is optional and shown that way (App Review 2.4.5, 2026-11-17): the welcome's Permissions step has Microphone and Sound from your Mac first, then a separate **Optional** block with Automatic pasting (**Turn On…**, was **Allow…**) and iCloud, with the line "Band·je works fully without it. Without automatic pasting, dictated text is on the clipboard and you paste it yourself with ⌘V." Settings › Access has the same **Optional** card. The message at launch no longer lists automatic pasting, and a missing paste permission no longer brings the welcome back after an update. Band·je only asks for the permission when you choose to turn it on (welcome, menu or Settings). **Warn at launch when something is missing** (on by default, now only the microphone and the sound of your Mac) moved into that Optional card. Without the permission a dictation no longer plays the error sound (Funk); it still does when secure input blocks pasting. The welcome's dictation shortcut then says the text is on the clipboard and you paste it with ⌘V, and after a dictation that wasn't pasted (permission off, or secure input) ⌘V shows next to the menu bar cassette for 3 seconds (owner).
- System audio permission (owner, after a tester's first call recording lacked the other side): Band·je now checks it itself. It plays an inaudible signal (-70 dB, muted by the check's own tap, on the built-in speakers when there are some, so AirPods are never woken or switched) for half a second and looks whether it comes in: macOS hands Band·je only silence while the permission is off (measured on macOS 26, also with the sound muted or at volume 0). **Sound from your Mac** in the welcome and Settings › Access (now right under Microphone, before Automatic pasting) has **Allow…**, which makes macOS ask right away instead of at the first recording, a check mark once allowed, and **Open Settings…** (then **Restart Band·je**) when off; after Open Settings it looks again every 3 seconds. At the start of every recording the check runs once; when the permission is off a warning says so right away (**Open Settings…** or **Continue with microphone only**); the recording goes on. On a first recording, while macOS's own question is open, it waits up to 30 seconds for the answer before warning. While recording, when nothing of the Mac's sound came in for 15 seconds while another app plays sound, it checks again (at most once a minute, not after a passed check). A recording started before the permission is known (the first one) first lets macOS ask and waits for the answer: started while macOS's question was open, it failed (error 268451843). At launch the missing-permission message includes the sound of your Mac when the last check said it was off, checked again first.

## 1.0.0

First App Store version: recording (microphone + system audio), on-device transcription with
speaker labels in 25 languages, speaker naming, dictation, interactive recording page, summaries
with Apple Intelligence (macOS 26), help in English and Dutch, app in 10 languages.
